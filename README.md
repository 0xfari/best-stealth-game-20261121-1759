**Lamplight Heist** is a top-down stealth game (pure canvas + ES modules, zero libraries, zero network): a thief robs a gaslit museum at night across 3 wings — The Foyer, The Gallery, and The Vault Wing. Guards patrol their rounds with vision cones that shift from yellow to orange to red as suspicion grows; a suspicion meter fills when you are caught in a cone (faster up close, in lamplight, and while walking) and drains when you slip back into shadow. Break line of sight and an alerted guard will search your last-seen spot, then give up and return to patrol.

Each wing hides 3 artifacts — the Jade Fox, the Moon Pearl, the Gilded Mask. Take them all and the exit unlocks; reach it unseen to clear the wing. Walking makes noise that guards come to investigate, so sneak when it matters: WASD or arrow keys move, Shift sneaks (slow, silent, harder to spot), P pauses, M mutes. You have 3 lives — get caught and the wing restarts with that wing's gains lost. Clear a wing fast for a time bonus, and finish a wing without ever being spotted for a +250 ghost bonus. All sound is synthesised in WebAudio: footsteps, guard treads that pan with distance and direction, the alert sting, the pickup chime, and the win fanfare.
